Arkham Halloween Photo Scavenger Hunt

A themed photo scavenger hunt for parties. Players solve riddles by photographing the answers around the venue on their phones, and moderators rule on each photo live, with verdicts in the style of Batman: Arkham: RIDDLE SOLVED, SUBJECT OBSCURED, SUBJECT TOO SMALL, MISALIGNED, SUBJECT NOT FOUND.

What it looks like

For players

  • Scan the join QR, pick a codename, and play. No account.
  • An installable app, built for the phone camera.
  • Teams share an evidence drawer, and invite each other with a link.
  • Live standings, or a final reveal when the round closes, then a recap of the night.

For hosts and moderators

  • A live queue: each photo gets a verdict, and players see it in seconds.
  • A conduct system with strikes, cooldowns and reversals, kept plain rather than themed.
  • Duplicate-photo detection, and a moderation log with the photo behind every call.
  • Print the join QR, replace a leaked code, reopen a round closed too early.

Run your own

One process and one data directory: FastAPI and SQLite behind a Preact PWA, with no other services to run. Deploy it as a container on a laptop at the venue, as a container behind a TLS proxy, or as a systemd service behind nginx. Single sign-on for moderators works with any OIDC provider that sends group names. deploy/README.md is the place to start.
